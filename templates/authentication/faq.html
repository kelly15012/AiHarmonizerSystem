Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Harmonizer?

AI Harmonizer is a tool that helps musicians generate chord progressions based on different genres, keys, and instruments.

How does AI Harmonizer work?

AI Harmonizer uses machine learning algorithms to analyze musical patterns and generate chord sequences that fit the selected parameters.

Can I customize the generated chords?

Yes, you can select the base key, genre, and specific chords to customize the generated chord progression.

What instruments are supported?

AI Harmonizer currently supports piano, guitar, and cello. More instruments will be added in future updates.

Is AI Harmonizer free to use?

Yes, AI Harmonizer offers a free version with basic features. Premium features are available through a subscription plan.
